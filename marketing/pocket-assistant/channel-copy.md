## Launch post (~150 words)

> A Kindle is calm by design. It is also, by design, just a screen that
> reads. PhoneKit closes that gap without turning the device into a noisy
> phone.
>
> In one KUAL extension you get a full-screen clock, a countdown timer that
> flashes the screen when time is up, a chat page that talks to any
> OpenAI-compatible endpoint — Ollama on the device, llama.cpp, vLLM, OpenAI,
> or OpenRouter — and an optional on-screen XMPP bridge that connects to your
> server over TLS and authenticates with SCRAM-SHA-1.
>
> Everything is plain shell and Python from the standard library. No compiled
> code, no installer, no bloat. It lives in one folder under KUAL and is gone
> when you delete that folder.
>
> It needs a jailbroken Kindle and KUAL. If you run KUAL, it is a five-minute
> install. Try it.
